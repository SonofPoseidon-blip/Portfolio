Compliance Management System

Most businesses manage compliance reactively. A deadline gets missed. A license expires. A regulator shows up.

I built a system that stays ahead of all of it — automatically.

  • Automated Risk-Scoring: Deadlines are dynamically color-coded (Red/Yellow/Green) so you can prioritize the most critical risks instantly.
  • Persistent Accountability: The system follows up via WhatsApp and Email until tasks are completed and verifiable proof is uploaded.
  • Immutable Audit Trail: Documentation is automatically centralized and cycles reset instantly, so you're always prepared for auditors.

Social Media Production Workflow

Fragmented chats and documents break content operations. I build structured pipelines that treat content like a high-velocity supply chain.

  • Eliminating Bottlenecks: A multi-stage pipeline (Ideation → Review → Approval → Scheduled) prevents draft limbo and ensures every post is vetted.
  • Role-Based Accountability: Clear ownership visibility means everyone knows their next step, eliminating the need for constant status meetings.
  • Centralized Creative Assets: Feedback and final files live in one hub, creating a library for easy repurposing and brand consistency.
